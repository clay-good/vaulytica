# add-mandated-text-conformance

## Why

Some clauses are not drafted; they are copied. A statute or regulation fixes the words, sometimes the type size, and a document either reproduces them or it does not. Today the engine checks these the way it checks everything else, by presence patterns, which is the wrong instrument: a presence pattern passes a notice that paraphrases, abbreviates, or quotes a superseded version of the text. The one place the engine does the right thing is the Texas privacy notice, where `TX_SENSITIVE_SALE_NOTICE` is matched whitespace-normalized and case-sensitively and an altered rendering is reported as "present but altered". That mechanism should be a table, not a special case.

The superseded-quotation case is the sharpest. California Civil Code § 1542 was amended by SB 1431 (Stats. 2018, ch. 157), effective January 1, 2019: "creditor" became "creditor or releasing party", "debtor" became "debtor or released party", "which" became "that", and "must have materially affected" became "would have materially affected". Firms' templates still carry the pre-2019 sentence, and commentators advised updating it to avoid a challenge to the waiver. A release signed in 2026 that quotes the 2018 text is a defect no presence rule can see, because the clause is present. Only a text comparison against the current statute sees it.

Beside exact text sit two neighbours the same table can carry: **form requirements** on the text (the Holder Rule notice must be "in at least ten point, bold face, type"; the door-to-door cancellation statement "in bold face type of a minimum size of 10 points"; a UCC § 2-316 warranty disclaimer must be conspicuous), which the DOCX ingest can partly read because it already carries bold runs; and the **conspicuousness of the document's own disclaimers**, which today `COMM-206` checks by presence of capitalized or bold-indicating words in text, without reading the run formatting a DOCX carries.

## What Changes

- **A `mandated_texts` DKB table.** One row per mandated passage: id, authority, the exact text as read at its official source, `source_url`, `retrieved_at`, the version date, any superseded text with its date range, the document families or assertion gates it applies to, and the form requirement if any (minimum point size, bold, capitals, "conspicuous"). Every row has a "read where" provenance; a row without one fails the DKB build.
- **Three rule shapes over the table**, `TEXT-*`, in the presence factory's style:
  - *present but altered*: the document contains a passage that matches the mandated text at a high similarity (≥ 0.85 token overlap in a sliding window) but not exactly; the finding shows a word-level redline of the document's text against the statute's.
  - *superseded version*: the passage matches a superseded text more closely than the current one; the finding names the amendment and date.
  - *form not met*: the passage is found but the DOCX run properties show it is not bold / not in capitals where the authority requires it, or the point size is below the minimum (DOCX `w:sz` is available; PDF font size is available from the text item transform). Reported only when the carrier exposes the property; otherwise "form not checked (PDF text layer / pasted text)".
- **Seed rows, each read at its official source during the build and cited in the row:** Cal. Civ. Code § 1542 (current and the pre-2019 text); 16 C.F.R. § 433.2(a) and (b) Holder Rule notices; 16 C.F.R. § 429.1(a) cancellation statement and § 429.1(b) Notice of Cancellation form; Tex. Bus. & Com. Code § 541.102(b)–(c) (migrated from `regime-data.ts`); 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b)(3) immunity-notice elements (as element presence, since the statute mandates content, not words). Scope rows: release/settlement families for § 1542; consumer-credit and retail-installment families for § 433; door-to-door, solar and home-improvement families for § 429; PIIA and employment families for § 1833(b).
- **Conspicuousness** as the fourth shape, `TEXT-FORM-*`: a warranty disclaimer or limitation of liability the DOCX carries entirely in mixed-case regular runs is reported as "not set in capitals or bold", citing U.C.C. § 1-201(b)(10) and § 2-316(2) as the standard the court applies, and never concluding the clause is unenforceable.
- **Reach:** the rules are ordinary engine rules, so every surface carries them. The redline uses the clause-diff word-diff renderer the comparison report already has.
- **No double firing.** Presence rules that already look for these passages (`COMM-206` for conspicuousness, the § 429.1 rules in `v7/residential-solar.ts`, the § 1833(b) notice rules in `v4/ip-licensing/_helpers.ts`, the § 1542 pattern in `v5/banking.ts`, the Texas exact-wording rules) are either retired in favour of the table row or kept as the absence check with the table row owning the altered/superseded/form checks. Each is decided per rule in the tasks and recorded in the CHANGELOG.

## Impact

- Affected specs: `mandated-text` (new capability; the DKB table and its provenance gate are specified inside it)
- Affected code: `dkb/` fetch and build for the table; `src/engine/rules/text/` factory; `src/privacy/regime-data.ts` loses its two literal notices to the table; DOCX ingest exposes `w:sz`, `w:caps`, `w:b` on runs (it already carries bold); `rule-completeness` and citation-currency guards extended
- Risk: `dkb_version` moves, so every golden re-baselines once; land with the next scheduled DKB rebuild. New findings on the corpus are measured and listed before merge.
