# add-redaction-integrity-scan

## Why

A redaction that can be lifted is the most public kind of disclosure failure. A black rectangle drawn over a PDF's text layer, or black highlighting applied in Word before "Save as PDF", hides the words from a reader and leaves them selectable, searchable, and one copy-paste from exposure. The failure class is old and still live: a Princeton study found 194 PACER filings with recoverable "redacted" text among 1.8 million examined; a Jones Day filing in W.D. Va. drew a show-cause order after grand-jury material was recovered by copy-paste; and in September 2025 Eugene Volokh reported the same failure in a fresh filing by an AmLaw-20 firm. The Free Law Project's `x-ray` exists for exactly this and runs server-side over public dockets, after the harm.

Vaulytica already reads the original container bytes for the pre-disclosure scan (`HANDOFF-001…005`): tracked changes, comments, hidden runs, metadata, PII. The production-QA pack's own scope statement says, in so many words, that it does not check "redaction integrity — whether a visual redaction is burned in or can be lifted." The document a lawyer must never upload to a cloud redaction checker is a privileged, partially redacted one. That is the no-server posture's strongest case, and the one read the delivery scan does not yet make.

A second, smaller half belongs beside it. An SEC filer may redact a material contract under Reg. S-K Item 601(b)(10)(iv) without a confidential-treatment request, but only if the filed exhibit marks each omission with brackets and carries a prominent first-page statement that information was excluded because it is both not material and of the kind the registrant customarily and actually treats as private or confidential (17 C.F.R. § 229.601(b)(10)(iv), read 2026-10-19 at law.cornell.edu). The EDGAR routing sample's 327 exhibits are the population this applies to (the sample holds routes and metadata, not text, so the check is measured on exhibits fetched for the purpose, as 9.948.0 did); a deterministic check that a bracketed omission has its legend is a presence check over text the engine already has.

## What Changes

- **A sixth and seventh delivery check**, on the same `ContainerFacts` read and under the same `delivery_hash`:
  - `HANDOFF-006` **Liftable redaction (PDF).** A filled rectangle in the page content stream (or a `Square`/`Redact`-without-apply annotation) whose box overlaps one or more text items on the same page, where the rectangle is a single opaque color. Reports page, box, and the **masked** count and shape of the covered text, never the covered text itself. The same masking invariant as `HANDOFF-005`.
  - `HANDOFF-007` **Visual-only redaction (DOCX).** A run whose text color equals its highlight or paragraph shading (black on black, white on white), or a run with a solid shading fill over text. Reports the location and a masked length; never the text.
- **A declared-redaction consistency note**, `HANDOFF-008`: a document that marks omissions with bracketed asterisks or the phrase "[omitted]" / "[redacted]" / "[\*\*\*]" but carries no first-page omission statement, when the document is an SEC exhibit (routed to a family that files on EDGAR, or asserted with `--sec-exhibit`). Presence-only: it says the legend was not found, never that the redaction was improper.
- **Reach:** every surface the delivery scan already reaches (tab card "Clean to send?", `--delivery`, JSON `delivery`, DOCX/HTML section, SARIF container results, bundle per-document card), plus `--fail-on-delivery` gating. The MCP server carries no delivery block today; `analyze_document` gains `include: ["delivery"]`, which is new work and is specced here because an agent linting a folder of redacted productions is the case that needs it.
- **Honesty:** a document with no text layer, an image-only redaction, or a PDF the reader could not parse to the operator level reports `inspectable: false` with a reason. A scan that finds nothing says "no liftable redaction of these shapes was found", never "redactions are safe".

## Impact

- Affected specs: `redaction-integrity` (new capability); the production-QA scope statement in `src/production/report.ts` (the sentence that disclaims redaction integrity is narrowed to what still is not checked: image-layer redactions and OCR'd scans)
- Affected code: `src/delivery/container.ts` (PDF operator walk via `page.getOperatorList()` and DOCX `w:rPr` color/highlight/shading read), `src/delivery/types.ts` (`RedactionFact`), `src/delivery/handoff.ts` (`HANDOFF-006…008`, beside 001–005), renderers that already carry the delivery block, `tools/mcp/tools.ts` (the `include: ["delivery"]` option; no new tool), `known-limits` ledger entry for the image-only class
- Risk: zero `result_hash` churn by construction (delivery is namespaced). The PDF operator walk is the one new cost; it is bounded by the existing page cap and the `MAX_OCR_PAGES`-style constant, measured before shipping.
