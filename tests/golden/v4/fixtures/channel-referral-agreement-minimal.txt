Referral Agreement

This Referral Agreement is entered into between Company, Brightpath Software Inc., and Partner, Meridian Advisors LLC.

Qualified Referral. A Qualified Referral means a prospect the Partner introduces who was not previously known to the Company and who executes an order within twelve (12) months of the introduction.

Referral Fee. The Company shall pay a referral fee of ten percent (10%) of the first-year contract value of each Qualified Referral, payable within thirty (30) days after the Company collects payment. The fee is subject to clawback on a refund or cancellation.

Independent Contractor. The Partner is an independent contractor and is not an agent, employee, or joint venturer of the Company, and has no authority to bind the Company, quote prices, or make representations on the Company's behalf.

Anti-Bribery. The Partner shall comply with the FCPA and applicable anti-corruption laws, shall make no improper payments or kickbacks, and the Company may terminate immediately on any violation.

Term. This Agreement continues for two (2) years and may be terminated by either party on thirty (30) days' notice.

Confidentiality. Each party shall keep confidential the referred leads, customer lists, and the other party's proprietary information, using them only for the referral relationship.

Non-Circumvention. Neither party shall circumvent the other or deal directly with a relationship introduced under this Agreement to avoid the Referral Fee for a period of two (2) years.
Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
