Master Supply Agreement

This Master Supply Agreement is entered into between Seller, Precision Components Inc., and Buyer, Acme Assembly Co., for the manufacture and supply of the Goods.

Quantity. Buyer shall purchase its requirements of the Goods from Seller during the Term, with a binding forecast delivered ninety (90) days in advance and a commitment for the first thirty (30) days of each forecast.

Delivery. Seller shall deliver the Goods FOB Seller's plant per the delivery schedule, with a standard lead time of fourteen (14) days. Risk of loss passes to Buyer on delivery to the carrier.

Specifications. The Goods shall conform to the Specifications attached as Exhibit A, and Seller warrants that the Goods will be free from defects in material and workmanship for twelve (12) months.

Price. The unit price is set on a cost-plus basis and is subject to annual price adjustment tied to the producer price index.

Inspection and Acceptance. Buyer may inspect the Goods within ten (10) days of delivery and reject nonconforming goods, which Seller shall replace or refund.

Force Majeure. Neither party shall be liable for any failure or delay in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of god, war, and supply-chain disruption; the affected party shall give prompt notice.

Best Efforts. Because Buyer purchases its requirements exclusively from Seller, Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to supply Buyer's requirements and Buyer shall use commercially reasonable efforts to purchase.

Term. This Agreement continues for three (3) years and renews for successive one-year terms unless either party gives sixty (60) days' notice of non-renewal.

Warranty of Title. Seller conveys good title to the Goods, free and clear of all liens and encumbrances, and warrants that the Goods do not infringe any third-party patent, trademark, or copyright; Seller shall provide an infringement indemnity.

Remedies. Repair or replacement of nonconforming Goods, or a refund, is Buyer's sole and exclusive remedy, provided that if that remedy fails of its essential purpose the full remedies under the UCC apply.
Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Ohio.
