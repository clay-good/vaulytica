Referral Agreement

This Referral Agreement is entered into between Company, Harbor Mortgage LLC, and Partner, Meridian Realty Advisors LLC.

Qualified Referral. A Qualified Referral means a homebuyer the Partner refers to the Company for mortgage loan origination and title insurance settlement services.

Referral Fee. The Company shall pay the Partner a referral fee of $500 for each closed mortgage loan, payable within thirty (30) days of closing.

Independent Contractor. The Partner is an independent contractor and has no authority to bind the Company.

Anti-Bribery. The Partner shall comply with the FCPA and applicable anti-corruption laws and make no improper payments.

Term. This Agreement continues for two (2) years. Governing Law: Delaware.
