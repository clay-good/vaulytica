Master Supply Agreement

This Master Supply Agreement is entered into between Seller, Precision Components Inc., and Buyer, Acme Assembly Co., for the manufacture and supply of the Goods.

Delivery. Seller shall deliver the Goods FOB Seller's plant per the delivery schedule, with a standard lead time of fourteen (14) days.

Specifications. The Goods shall conform to the Specifications and Seller warrants that the Goods will be free from defects in material and workmanship.

Price. The unit price is set on a cost-plus basis with annual price adjustment.

Inspection and Acceptance. Buyer may inspect the Goods and reject nonconforming goods.

Force Majeure. Neither party is liable for events beyond its reasonable control.

Term. This Agreement continues for three (3) years. Governing Law: Ohio.
