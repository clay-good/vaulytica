Marketing Services Agreement

This Marketing Services Agreement is entered into between Client, Verde Foods Inc., and Agency, Loud Harbor Creative LLC.

Scope of Work. The Agency shall provide the marketing services and deliverables described in the Statement of Work, including creative development, an influencer campaign, and an email newsletter campaign for the Products.

Endorsements. The Agency shall require every influencer and endorser to clearly and conspicuously disclose the material connection with the Client (for example, "#ad" or "paid partnership"), consistent with the FTC endorsement guides at 16 C.F.R. Part 255.

Email. All commercial email shall comply with CAN-SPAM: accurate header and subject lines, a functioning unsubscribe honored within ten (10) business days, and a valid physical postal address.

Ownership. All deliverables and work product are works made for hire owned by the Client, and to the extent any deliverable is not a work made for hire the Agency assigns all right, title, and interest to the Client on payment.

Claims. All advertising claims shall be truthful, non-deceptive, and substantiated before use, and the Agency shall maintain the substantiation file.

Fees. The Client shall pay the fees set out in the Statement of Work within thirty (30) days of invoice.

Term. This Agreement continues for one (1) year and renews for successive one-year terms unless either party gives sixty (60) days' notice.

Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York.
