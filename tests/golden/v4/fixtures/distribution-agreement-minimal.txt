Distribution Agreement

This Distribution Agreement is entered into between Supplier, Nimbus Brands LLC, and Distributor, Coastline Sales Corp.

Appointment. Supplier appoints Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products in the Territory of the western United States. Distributor shall not sell outside the Territory.

Minimum Purchases. Distributor shall purchase an annual minimum of 25,000 units; failure to meet the minimum converts the appointment to non-exclusive.

Resale Prices. Distributor is free to determine its own resale prices. Any pricing guidance is limited to a unilateral minimum advertised price (MAP) policy.

Trademark License. Supplier grants Distributor a limited, revocable license to use the Trademarks in the Territory, subject to Supplier's quality control and right to inspect and approve use.

Term and Termination. The term is three (3) years. Either party may terminate for cause upon a material breach that remains uncured for thirty (30) days, and Supplier may terminate for convenience on ninety (90) days' prior written notice.

Post-Termination. On termination, Supplier shall repurchase Distributor's unsold inventory at net invoice cost, and Distributor shall cease using the marks and return all branded materials and samples.

Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois.
