Conflict of Interest Policy

Adopted by the Board of Directors of Thornapple Instruments Corporation on January 21, 2026.

Purpose and Scope. This Conflict of Interest Policy applies to every director, officer, and key employee of the Corporation.

Definition. A conflict of interest arises when a covered person, or a family member of a covered person, has a financial interest or a business relationship with a party that is or seeks to be a related party to a transaction with the Corporation, or otherwise has an interest that could impair independent judgment.

Annual Disclosure. Each covered person shall complete an annual disclosure statement and an annual certification listing every financial interest and business relationship that could give rise to a conflict of interest. The duty is a continuing duty: a covered person shall update the disclosure promptly when circumstances change.

Recusal and Review. A covered person with a conflict shall recuse themselves from the deliberation and the vote. The disinterested and independent directors shall review the related party transaction and determine, by a vote of a majority of them, whether it is in the Corporation's best interest and whether a more advantageous arrangement is reasonably available. The determination and the basis for it shall be recorded in the minutes, and approval requires the affirmative vote of a majority of the disinterested directors.

Sanctions for Violations. A violation of this policy is grounds for discipline, including disciplinary action up to removal from the Board or termination of employment. The Audit Committee shall determine the appropriate consequences after notice to the covered person and an opportunity to respond.
