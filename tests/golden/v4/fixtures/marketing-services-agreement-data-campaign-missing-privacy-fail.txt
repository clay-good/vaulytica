Marketing Services Agreement

This Marketing Services Agreement is entered into between Client, Verde Foods Inc., and Agency, Loud Harbor Creative LLC.

Scope of Work. The Agency shall provide marketing services and deliverables for the Campaign, including managing the Client's email list and consumer personal data for audience segmentation and retargeting.

Endorsements. All influencers shall clearly and conspicuously disclose any material connection (#ad) in accordance with 16 C.F.R. Part 255.

Email. All commercial email shall comply with CAN-SPAM: accurate headers, a working unsubscribe mechanism, and a valid physical postal address.

Ownership. All deliverables and work product are works made for hire owned by the Client, and the Agency assigns all right, title, and interest to the Client.

Approvals. The Client's prior written approval is required for all creative, media plans, and public claims before publication.

Claims. All advertising claims shall be truthful, non-deceptive, and substantiated before use.

Fees. The Client shall pay the fees in the Statement of Work within thirty (30) days of invoice.

Term. This Agreement continues for one (1) year. Governing Law: New York.
